Who We Are

Engineering Intelligence Into Every Building System

Servomatrix is a specialist building automation and controls engineering firm. We design, integrate, program, commission, and support the full spectrum of BMS infrastructure — from a single DDC controller to enterprise-wide smart building networks.

Our engineers operate at the intersection of mechanical, electrical, and digital systems — bridging the gap between field devices and high-level building intelligence platforms.

What We Do

Core Engineering Services

Comprehensive building automation capabilities delivered by engineers who understand both the field and the code.

01

Building Management Systems

Full BMS design, specification, and integration. We architect centralised control platforms that give operators real-time visibility and control across all mechanical and electrical systems.

02

HVAC Controls Engineering

Precision control logic for AHUs, FCUs, chillers, cooling towers, boilers, and VAV systems. Sequences engineered to optimise comfort, energy use, and equipment longevity.

03

DDC Panel Engineering

Custom DDC panel design and fabrication — from schematic drawings through to I/O schedules, panel build, testing, and documentation. Built to specification, built to last.

04

BACnet & Modbus Integration

Seamless protocol integration across disparate building systems. We configure, map, and validate BACnet IP/MSTP and Modbus RTU/TCP networks with full point list documentation.

05

Energy Monitoring

Sub-metering design, meter integration, and energy dashboards that expose consumption patterns, identify waste, and support compliance with energy reporting requirements.

06

Smart Building Dashboards

Web-based operator interfaces and analytics dashboards — configurable views, alarm management, trend logging, and remote access engineered for facility management teams.

07

BMS Programming

Controller programming across major platforms including Niagara Framework, EcoStruxure, OPUS, Trend IQ, and Distech ECB. Logic that runs correctly from day one.

08

Commissioning & Testing

Systematic pre-commissioning checks, functional performance testing, and handover documentation — ensuring every point is verified and every sequence is proven before practical completion.

09

Technical Support & Maintenance

Ongoing BMS support, remote diagnostics, fault resolution, software updates, and preventive maintenance programs — keeping critical building systems operational around the clock.

How We Work

A Disciplined Engineering Process

Every Servomatrix engagement follows a structured methodology from brief to handover.

01

Brief & Requirements

We define scope, system boundaries, protocol requirements, and performance criteria with the project team. No assumptions — everything documented from the outset.

02

Engineering Design

DDC architecture, network diagrams, I/O schedules, panel schematics, and control sequence descriptions — complete design documentation before any fabrication or programming begins.

03

Build & Programme

Panel fabrication, controller configuration, software programming, and bench testing completed in a controlled environment before site delivery.

04

Commission & Verify

Systematic point-by-point verification, sequence testing, and functional performance testing with the mechanical and electrical teams. Every alarm, every setpoint, every interlock confirmed.

05

Handover & Support

Full O&M documentation, operator training, and transition to ongoing support arrangements. Systems handed over ready to operate — not to debug.
